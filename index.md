# Privacy Policy for Grandad's Wordle

**Effective Date:** July 2026

Grandad's Wordle is an offline word game developed by Neil Mouat.

## Information Collection

Grandad's Wordle does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app:
- Does not require a user account.
- Does not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Does not collect location information.
- Does not collect usage analytics.
- Does not use cookies.
- Does not contain advertising.

## Internet Access

Grandad's Wordle is designed to work offline. No personal information is sent to the developer or to third parties while using the app.

## Children's Privacy

Grandad's Wordle does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy is updated in the future, the latest version will be made available with the app.
